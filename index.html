About Luvio HealthLink

Your Personal Health Architecture.

Luvio HealthLink is being designed as a digital healthcare ecosystem rather than simply a clinic management system.

Its purpose is to create a connected healthcare journey where patients, healthcare professionals and healthcare organizations can interact through appropriate digital workflows.

The platform is intended to grow from a single-polyclinic implementation into a wider healthcare network.

HealthLink Architecture

  • 👤 Patient-centred health record
  • 🩺 Doctor consultation workflow
  • 🏥 Polyclinic operations
  • 💊 Connected pharmacy services
  • 🧪 Diagnostic integration
  • 🏨 Hospital referral connectivity
  • 🔐 Consent-based information sharing
  • 🌐 Future ABDM / FHIR interoperability

Building Connected Healthcare

Luvio HealthLink is being developed step by step — beginning with a focused single-polyclinic implementation and growing toward a connected healthcare network.
