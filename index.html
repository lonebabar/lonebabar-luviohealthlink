How Luvio HealthLink Works

A connected healthcare journey built around the patient.

1

Patient Registration

A patient receives a unique Luvio Patient ID and their basic healthcare profile is created.

2

Consultation

The doctor accesses the authorized patient information and records the consultation digitally.

3

Connected Services

Prescriptions, diagnostic orders and referrals can be routed to authorized connected providers.

4

Patient Record

Relevant consultation, prescription and report information becomes part of the patient's longitudinal record.
