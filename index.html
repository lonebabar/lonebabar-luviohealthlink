HOW LUVIO HEALTHLINK WORKS

Connected Without Losing Clear Ownership

Patient identity, professional identity and organization identity are separate. Authorized relationships connect them.

1

Patient Identity

The patient receives a unique Luvio Patient ID and uses secure authentication to access their record.

2

Professional Identity

Doctors and other professionals register independently and can work with multiple organizations.

3

Organization Workspace

Each organization manages its own operations, staff, services and authorized professional associations.

4

Connected Care

With appropriate authorization, consultations, prescriptions, diagnostics and referrals can move through the ecosystem.

ABOUT LUVIO HEALTHLINK

Your Personal Health Architecture.

Luvio HealthLink is designed as a digital healthcare ecosystem rather than a single clinic-management application.

The platform can start with one polyclinic, prove the workflow, and progressively connect additional organizations and professionals.

  • Patient-centric longitudinal health record
  • Independent professional identity
  • Organization-based operational workspaces
  • Explicit professional-to-organization associations
  • Connected pharmacy, diagnostics and hospital workflows
  • Online consultation
  • Future interoperability and authorized data sharing

Built to grow

The main interface remains simple while the platform underneath can expand into multiple healthcare organizations, professional networks and connected services.

Important: A standalone pharmacy can operate its own complete pharmacy software without being a polyclinic. A pharmacy may also be associated with a polyclinic when required.
