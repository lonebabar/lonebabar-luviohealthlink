One Connected Ecosystem

Luvio HealthLink is designed to connect the different parts of a patient's healthcare journey.

👤

Patient

Access appointments, prescriptions, reports and healthcare information through a connected digital record.

🩺

Doctor

View patient history, record consultations, prescribe medicines, order tests and create referrals.

🏥

Polyclinic

Manage reception, patient registration, queues, consultations and clinic operations.

💊

Pharmacy

Receive authorized digital prescriptions and maintain dispensing information.

🧪

Diagnostics

Receive diagnostic orders and return laboratory reports directly into the patient's record.

🏨

Partner Hospitals

Receive digital referrals and authorized clinical information while maintaining their own hospital systems.

How HealthLink Works

A simple workflow designed around the patient.

1

Register

Reception searches for an existing patient or creates a new patient record.

2

Consult

The doctor views relevant history, records the consultation and prepares the treatment plan.

3

Confirm

One final confirmation sends authorized prescriptions, diagnostic orders and referrals to the appropriate services.

4

Continue Care

Reports, medicines, referrals and future consultations become part of the longitudinal patient record.

HealthLink Portals

👤 Patient Portal

Healthcare, Connected.
