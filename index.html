ABOUT LUVIO HEALTHLINK

Your Personal Health Architecture.

Luvio HealthLink is designed to create a connected digital healthcare environment where the patient's journey remains at the centre.

The platform is designed to grow from a single polyclinic into a wider network of participating healthcare providers.

  • Patient-centric healthcare
  • Longitudinal digital health record
  • Connected healthcare providers
  • Secure and authorized data sharing
  • Designed for future interoperability

Built around the patient

Luvio HealthLink is not simply a clinic management system. It is designed as a healthcare ecosystem connecting patients, healthcare professionals, clinics, pharmacies, diagnostics and hospitals.

The platform can start small, prove the workflow in one polyclinic and progressively expand into a broader healthcare network.
